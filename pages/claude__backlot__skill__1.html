How it works

Three scenes, and the paperwork runs itself.

SCENE 01

Drop the deal in

Forward the sponsor email or upload the PDF. Backlot reads out the fee, the deliverables, the due date and the exclusivity window, then asks you to confirm the three it is least sure about.

SCENE 02

Backlot runs the board

Deliverables become dated cards. Invoices go out the moment you mark a video delivered. Chasers send themselves on day 7, 14 and 30 in your words, not a template's.

SCENE 03

Money lands and gets logged

Sponsor transfers and platform payouts match themselves against what you were owed. Anything unmatched sits in one short list instead of hiding in a bank feed.
