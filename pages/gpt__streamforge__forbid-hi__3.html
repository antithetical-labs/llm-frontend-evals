How it works

More useful than another highlight reel.

Streamforge captures the whole engineering process—not just the polished result. Watch experienced developers reason, prompt, debug, and ship in real time.

01

Connect your workspace

Link a repository and your preferred AI coding tools. Streamforge organizes the session without changing how you work.

02

Build in the open

Your audience follows the prompt thread, model output, active files, terminal events, and commits as they happen.

03

Fork the result

Every stream stays connected to a reproducible repository. Viewers can fork from a meaningful checkpoint and continue building.
