HOW IT WORKS

01

Go live

Connect your editor and your AI assistant. Your prompts, completions, diffs, and terminal stream as one synchronized feed. No setup beyond a single CLI command.

02

Build in public

Viewers watch your reasoning unfold — the prompt you wrote, what the model returned, what you accepted, rejected, and rewrote. Chat annotates specific lines in real time.

03

Fork & continue

Every stream backs a live repository. Any viewer can fork the exact state of the code at any timestamp and keep building from your commit history.

BUILT FOR OPERATORS

⟁

Prompt-transparent streams

The full loop is visible: your prompt, the raw model output, and the final edit side by side. Learn how top engineers actually talk to their models — not the highlight reel.

⎇

One-click repo forking

Every stream is a living git repo. Fork the codebase at any point in the broadcast, replay the diff history, and open a pull request against a stream that's still live.

⌘

Model-agnostic capture

Works with any assistant — copilots, chat agents, CLI coders. Streamforge normalizes every interaction into a unified, searchable transcript tied to each commit.
