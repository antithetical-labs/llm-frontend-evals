How it works

Three steps. About twenty minutes. Then it runs itself.

No spreadsheets to rebuild, no bookkeeping vocabulary to learn. If you can drop a PDF into a folder, you can run Backlot.

1

Drop in the deal

Forward the email or upload the contract PDF. Backlot pulls out the fee, the deliverables, the usage window and the payment terms for you to confirm.

2

Connect your platforms

Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon and your bank. Every payout lands on one timeline, labelled in plain English instead of transaction codes.

3

Get nudged, get paid

Backlot reminds you before a deliverable is due, invoices the brand on the right day, and chases politely when a payment goes quiet.
