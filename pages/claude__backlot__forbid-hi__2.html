How it works

Three steps. Then it mostly runs itself.

You're a creator, not a bookkeeper. Backlot is built so the setup takes one coffee and the upkeep takes one minute a week.

1

Drop in the deal

Forward the brand email or upload the PDF contract. Backlot pulls out the fee, the deliverables, the due dates and anything that limits who else you can work with.

forward → deals@yourname.backlot
2

Connect where you get paid

Add your platforms and your invoicing email. Backlot tracks each payout separately — YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon, affiliate links — with the date it actually arrives.

read-only · no bank credentials
3

Get told what's next

One short list every Monday: what to film, what to send, who's late, and what's landing this month. Invoices and polite chase-ups go out on their own.

Monday 9am · email or push
