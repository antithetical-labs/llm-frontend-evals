    How it works

    Three steps from your editor to an audience.

    No overlays, no editing bay, no highlight reels. You build exactly like you already do — we just open the door.

    01

    Go live with your assistant

    Install the bridge, point it at your editor and model. We capture your prompt stream, the completions, and your working diff — in perfect sync.

    Setup: 4 minutes
    02

    Build in the open

    Viewers watch the reasoning unfold in real time. They can replay any step, copy any prompt, and queue suggestions you approve with one keystroke.

    Zero edits. Ever.
    03

    Ship a forkable repo

    When you stop streaming, the repo lands with its full prompt history attached. Anyone can fork it and continue the build with the same context.
