// How it works

From prompt to production, in public.

Three steps between you and the most honest developer education on the internet.

01

Go live with your assistant

Connect your editor with forge stream and broadcast your session. Your prompts, the model's raw output, and your diffs stream side by side — nothing edited, nothing staged.

02

Viewers watch the real process

The audience sees how you actually steer the model: the retries, the rejected suggestions, the moments you take the keyboard back. Chat, ask questions, and suggest prompts in real time.

03

Fork the repo, keep building

Every stream is backed by a live repository. One click forks the exact state of the code at any timestamp — commits, branches, and the full prompt history included.

// Features

Built for developers who ship.

Not another highlight reel. Streamforge captures the whole loop — intent, generation, and judgment.

▸_

Prompt-level replay

Every stream is indexed by prompt. Scrub the timeline to the exact moment a function was generated, see the prompt that produced it, and compare the model's output against what actually shipped.

⑂

One-click repo forking

Fork any stream's repository at any point in the broadcast. You get the code, the commit graph, and the annotated prompt history — a running start instead of a blank main.

◉

Multi-model transparency

Streams are tagged with the exact model, version, and settings in use. Follow builders working with your stack and your assistant, and learn the prompting patterns that actually transfer.
