How it works

From inbox chaos to one clean ledger.

Three steps, about twenty minutes of setup. After that, Backlot runs the admin while you run the channel.

01

Plug in your platforms

Connect YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, and Instagram, then forward your brand-deal emails. Backlot reads payout statements and deal threads automatically.

02

Backlot files everything

Contracts get signed-status tracking, invoices get due dates, and every sponsor deliverable lands on one deadline calendar. Nothing lives in your inbox anymore.

03

Get paid on time

Late invoices trigger polite automatic nudges, payouts get matched to invoices, and you see exactly what’s earned, pending, and overdue — per platform.
