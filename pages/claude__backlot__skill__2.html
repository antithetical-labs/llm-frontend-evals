How it works

Three passes, then the board runs itself.

01

Drop the paperwork in

Forward the deal email or upload the contract PDF. Backlot reads out the fee, the deliverables, the exclusivity window and the payment terms, and shows you what it found before saving.

02

Connect where you get paid

Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon and your bank. Sponsor money and platform money land on the same timeline instead of four separate dashboards.

03

Work the strip board

Each deal is one strip: shoot date, delivery date, invoice date, paid date. Nothing sits in "sent and forgotten" because Backlot chases the ones that go quiet.

Set up takes about fifteen minutes. Most of that is finding the last contract you signed.

Ad revenue, subscriptions, tips and affiliate payments arrive on different days with different fees taken out. Backlot lines them up by month so you can see what a normal month looks like and plan for the thin one.
