Scene 02 / How it works

Three takes to a clean books.

No accounting degree required. If you can read a call sheet, you can run Backlot.

Take 1

Drop in the deal

Forward a contract or paste the terms. Backlot pulls out the fee, deliverables, usage rights, and payment terms, and files it under the brand.

Take 2

Track the work

Each deliverable gets a deadline and a status. Approvals, revisions, and publish dates stay attached to the deal that pays for them.

Take 3

Watch the money land

Invoices go out on schedule. Payouts from YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, and sponsors roll up into one income view you can actually read.
