How it works

From chaos to handled in one afternoon

Backlot meets you where you are — a folder of contracts, a pile of PayPal emails, and a notes app full of deadlines — and turns it into a clear picture of your business.

1

Drop in your deals

Forward a contract or paste the deal terms. Backlot pulls out the deliverables, rates, payment terms, and key dates automatically.

2

Deadlines for drafts, revisions, and go-live dates land in one timeline. Late invoices get flagged so you never chase money awkwardly — or forget to.
