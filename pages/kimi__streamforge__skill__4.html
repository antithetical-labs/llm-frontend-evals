// how it works

Your terminal is the stage

No overlays, no scene switching, no production setup. Streamforge pipes your editor, your prompts, and your git history into one watchable feed.

  1. Connect your repo

    Install the CLI and point it at a project. Streamforge tracks your branch, diffs, and commits as the stream's source of truth.

  2. Build in the open

    Every prompt you send and every completion you accept or reject shows up live, alongside the code it produces. Viewers see the real process, not a highlight reel.

  3. Let them fork it

    Any viewer can clone the repo at any commit from the stream timeline — including the exact prompt history that got it there.

Built for the way you actually work

Streamforge treats prompts as first-class artifacts. What you asked matters as much as what shipped.
