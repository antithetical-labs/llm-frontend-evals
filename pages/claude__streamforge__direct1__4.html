02 — protocol

Three steps.
Zero ceremony.

Streamforge hooks straight into your editor and your assistant. No OBS scenes, no scene-switching, no webcam required.

01

Pipe your session

Install the CLI, run one command, and your terminal, editor buffers and assistant transcript start broadcasting as structured events — not just pixels.

$ forge live --repo .
02

Viewers read the loop

The player shows three synced tracks: your prompts, the model's raw output, and the live diff. Scrub back to any token and watch the code re-assemble.

prompt ▸ output ▸ diff
03

Anyone forks it

Every stream carries its Git history. One click clones the repo at the exact commit on screen — including the prompt log that produced it.

⑂ fork @ 01:42:07
03 — capabilities

Built for people who ship.

P/O/D

Triple-track playback

Prompt, output and diff are recorded as separate time-aligned streams. Search a stream for useEffect and jump to the second it was written. Copy any prompt verbatim without pausing to squint at 720p video.

indexed · searchable
FORK

Fork any stream, any moment

Repos are first-class objects, not links in a description. Fork mid-broadcast and you get the working tree, the branch, the environment manifest and the full prompt transcript that got it there. Ship your own variant before the stream ends.

git-native · 1 click
LOOP

Chat that edits the loop

Viewers submit prompt suggestions straight into a queue beside your composer. Upvote, accept, or reject with a keystroke — the audience becomes a second pair of hands instead of a wall of noise. Full moderation and rate limits built in.

low latency · <400ms
