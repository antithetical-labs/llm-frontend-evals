01 / HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
Then it runs itself.

Setup takes about eleven minutes, most of which is you finding your logins. After that Backlot updates on its own and only speaks up when something needs you.

STEP 01

Drop in the deal

Forward the email or upload the PDF. Backlot reads the contract and pulls out the fee, the deliverables, the deadlines, the payment terms and the usage window — and tells you what any of that means in plain language.

STEP 02

Connect your payouts

Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon, Substack and your bank. Every platform's money lands in one timeline, converted, dated and labelled — instead of six dashboards and a guess.

STEP 03

Get told what's next

One daily brief: what to deliver, what to invoice, who's late, what's clearing. Invoices send themselves on the terms in the contract. Chasers go out without you writing an awkward email.
