the loop

How a stream works

No decks, no post-production. Just a developer, a model, and a repo evolving in front of you.

01 / broadcast

Go live with your assistant

Hit streamforge go in your terminal. Your editor, your prompts, and the model's raw output stream side-by-side. Nothing is edited, nothing is hidden.

02 / observe

Viewers watch the thinking

The audience sees the prompt before the code. They watch bad outputs get rejected, prompts get sharpened, and architecture decisions happen in chat in real time.

03 / fork

Take the repo and run

Every stream is backed by a live git repo. One click forks the exact state you're watching — commit history, branches, and the prompt log included.
