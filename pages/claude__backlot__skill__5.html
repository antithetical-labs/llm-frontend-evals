How it works

Three steps, in order. About twenty minutes.

  1. 01

    Drop in the deal

    Upload the contract PDF or paste the email thread. Backlot pulls out the fee, the payment terms, the deliverable list, the usage window and the exclusivity clause, then shows you what it found so you can correct it.

  2. 02

    Connect where the money lands

    Link your YouTube, TikTok, Twitch and Patreon payout accounts once. Ad revenue, tips and platform bonuses land on the same calendar as your sponsor invoices, in the currency you actually get paid in.

  3. 03

    Work the board

    Each morning you get one list: what ships today, what invoice to send, what payment is late and who to email about it. Nothing else asks for your attention.
