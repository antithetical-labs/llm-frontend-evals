/ HOW IT WORKS

From blank file to shipped feature, in the open

No polished tutorials, no edited highlight reels. You see the actual process: the dead ends, the refactors, the prompts that worked and the ones that didn't.

  1. 01

    Go live from your editor

    One CLI command streams your session: forge cast. Your prompts, model output, and file diffs broadcast side by side. Your keys and secrets never leave your machine.

  2. 02

    Viewers follow the reasoning

    The audience watches the conversation with the model in one pane and the evolving code in the other, with chat threaded to specific lines and prompts.

  3. 03

    Anyone can fork the repo

    Every stream is backed by a real repository. One click forks it to your account with full history, so you can rerun the prompts, change the approach, and make it yours.

/ FEATURES

Built for developers who learn by watching it done

⎇

Prompt-aware replay

Missed a stream? Scrub the replay by prompt, not by timestamp. Jump straight to the moment a design decision was made and see both the question and the code it produced.

⤓

One-click repo forks

Fork any live or past stream's repository with complete commit history and the full prompt transcript embedded as annotated metadata alongside the code.

❓

Line-level discussion

Ask questions pinned to an exact line or a specific prompt. Streamers answer live; on replays, answers surface for everyone who hits the same spot later.
