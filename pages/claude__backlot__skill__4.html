How it works

Three steps, then it runs in the background.

Setup takes about fifteen minutes. After that you only open Backlot when something needs signing, sending or chasing.

  1. 01

    Drop in the deal

    Forward the sponsor email or upload the contract PDF. Backlot reads out the fee, the deliverables, the exclusivity window and the payment terms, then shows you what it found so you can correct it.

  2. 02

    Connect the money

    Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon and your bank. Payouts land in one running ledger with the platform fee already separated out, so you can see what you actually earned per video.

  3. 03

    Work the sheet

    Each morning you get one short list: what ships today, what invoice to send, who is late. Chasers go out on their own unless you tell them not to.
