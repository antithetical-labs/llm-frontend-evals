Reel 01 — How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

You already do this work. Backlot just stops it from living in eleven browser tabs and a note on your phone called "money????".

STEP 01

Drop the contract in

Forward the PDF or paste the email. Backlot pulls out the fee, the deliverables, the deadlines, the exclusivity window and the payment terms — and shows you what it found before saving anything.

STEP 02

Connect where you get paid

YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon, plus your bank. Payouts land in one ledger with the platform, the date and the cut already labelled. No CSV surgery.

STEP 03

Work off one sheet

Every morning you get a short list: what ships today, who owes you, what to chase. Invoices send themselves. Follow-ups nudge without you writing the awkward email.
