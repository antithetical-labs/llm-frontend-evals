How it works

Three steps, then it mostly runs itself.

Backlot doesn't ask you to think like a finance person. It asks who the deal is with and when it ships.

1

Drop in the deal

Forward the sponsor email or upload the PDF. Backlot pulls out the fee, the deliverables, the due dates and the exclusivity window so you don't have to read clause 7 again.

2

Connect your money

Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon and your bank. Payouts are matched to the right deal or platform automatically, in one currency you choose.

3

Work off one list

Each morning you get a short list: what ships today, who owes you, what to send. Nudges to late sponsors go out on their own.

What you get
