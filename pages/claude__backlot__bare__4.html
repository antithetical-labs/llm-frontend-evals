How it works

Three steps. No spreadsheets, no accounting degree.

Backlot is built around how deals actually arrive — as a PDF in your inbox at 11pm.

01

Drop in the contract

Forward the email or upload the PDF. Backlot pulls out the fee, deliverables, usage window and exclusivity terms and shows them in plain English.

02

Connect your platforms

Link your channels and payment accounts once. Every AdSense, Creator Fund and Patreon deposit lands on the same timeline as your brand money.

03

Get told what's next

One weekly nudge: what to film, what to invoice, who's late. Chase-up emails are pre-written — you just hit send.
