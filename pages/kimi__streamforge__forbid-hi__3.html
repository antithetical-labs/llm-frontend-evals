How it works

Three panels. One honest workflow.

No highlight reels, no post-production. Streamforge shows the actual loop between a developer and their AI assistant as it happens.

01 / PROMPT

See the prompt, verbatim

Every instruction the streamer sends to their assistant is displayed as typed — including the bad ones. Watch how experienced engineers phrase, constrain, and retry prompts. The stream is piped straight from the editor via streamforge-cli.

02 / OUTPUT

Watch the code evolve

The model's response and the resulting diff land side by side, commit by commit. Accepts, rejects, and hand-edits are all visible, so you learn what to keep and what to throw away — not just the final green build.

03 / FORK

Fork any stream's repo

Every broadcast is backed by a live repository. Fork it at any commit mid-stream, follow along in your own editor, or pick up where the streamer left off after they sign off. One click, full history, prompts included.

Built for working developers

Learn the craft of AI-assisted engineering

Reading docs tells you what a model can do. Watching someone work tells you what it can't — and how to route around it.

Prompt-level transparency

The prompt pane is first-class, not an afterthought. Search a streamer's past broadcasts by the prompts they used, compare phrasing strategies, and build your own library of patterns that actually shipped.

→ searchable prompt archive

One-click repo forking

Every stream is a real repository with real history. Fork at any commit — before the fix, after the refactor, mid-experiment. CI status, branches, and the full prompt log travel with your fork.

→ git-native, zero lock-in

Chat with full context

Viewer questions are anchored to the exact prompt, completion, or commit being discussed. Ask "why did you reject that suggestion?" and the streamer sees the diff you're pointing at — not a wall of scrolling text.
