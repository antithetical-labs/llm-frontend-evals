01

How it works

Install the CLI, start a session, and Streamforge captures your prompts, model output, terminal, and repository state as one synchronised timeline. Viewers scrub it like a video and branch off it like a repo.

STEP 1

Pipe your session

streamforge start wraps your agent and editor. Secrets and env files are filtered before anything leaves your machine.

STEP 2

Go live

Three panes broadcast together: prompt log, model output, and the live diff. Sub-second latency, no compositing setup.

STEP 3

Viewers follow along

The chat is threaded onto commits, so "why did you accept that suggestion?" stays attached to the line it's about.

STEP 4

Anyone forks

One click clones the repo at the exact commit shown, prompt history included, ready to run locally.
