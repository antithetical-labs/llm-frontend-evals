How it works

Three steps. No finance degree required.

You already do this work — it's just scattered across email, Notes and five different dashboards. Backlot puts it on one screen.

1

Drop in the deal

Forward the sponsor email or upload the PDF. Backlot pulls out the fee, deliverables, usage window and payment terms so you can read them in plain English.

2

Connect your payouts

Link the platforms you actually get paid by. Every AdSense, Creator Fund and affiliate deposit lands in one running total, split by source.

3

Let it nag for you

Backlot reminds you before a deliverable is late and chases invoices that have gone quiet — with a polite follow-up you can send in one tap.
