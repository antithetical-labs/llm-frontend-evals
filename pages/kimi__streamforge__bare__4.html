// how_it_works

From blank repo to shipped feature, in the open.

No post-produced tutorials. No "and then I prepared this earlier." Every keystroke, every prompt, every hallucination — live.

01

Go live with your stack

Connect your editor, terminal, and AI assistant in one click. Streamforge captures your prompts, model responses, and file diffs as a unified timeline.

02

Viewers follow the reasoning

The audience sees not just the final code, but how you steered the model — what you rejected, what you refined, and why. Chat is threaded per prompt.

03

Anyone forks the repo

Every stream is backed by a real git repository, snapshotted per commit. One click forks the full history — prompts included as annotated commit messages.
