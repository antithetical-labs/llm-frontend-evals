The loop

Build. Broadcast. Branch.

Streamforge turns AI-assisted development into a shared, inspectable process—not a polished demo after the interesting work is over.

01 input.signal

Connect your build

Link a repository, start your editor, and bring the AI assistant you already use. Streamforge captures the working context.

02 broadcast.live

Ship the process

Viewers follow your camera, prompts, model responses, code changes, and terminal output as one synchronized stream.

03 repo.fork()

Let it branch

Every stream stays connected to its repository. Viewers can fork the exact state they are watching and keep building.
