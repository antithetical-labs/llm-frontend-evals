Plug in. Broadcast. Get forked.

One CLI command wires your editor, your terminal and your assistant into a single synchronized broadcast. No OBS scene graph, no window capture, no leaked API keys.

01

Pipe your session

npx streamforge go attaches to your repo and your assistant of choice. Prompts, completions, tool calls and file writes are captured as structured events — not pixels.

02

Viewers see everything

A three-pane player: prompt log, streaming model output, and a live diff of the working tree. Scrub back to any token to find the exact moment a decision was made.

03

Fork mid-broadcast

Any viewer hits ⑂ and gets the repo at that exact commit, plus the full prompt history as context. Diverge from the stream and ship your own version tonight.
