How it works

From forge live to a forkable repo, in one sitting.

  1. 01

    Go live with one command

    forge live shares your editor, your terminal, and your prompt log. Your repo mirrors to Streamforge automatically — every commit lands in the open, seconds after you make it.

  2. 02

    Pair in public

    Viewers see every prompt you send and every token the model returns. No highlight reels, no cuts, no "and then it worked." The mistakes are the good part.

  3. 03

    Ship a forkable repo

    The stream's repo updates with each commit. Anyone watching can fork it mid-build, pick up your thread, and open a PR before you've even ended the stream.
