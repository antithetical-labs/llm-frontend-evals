Protocol / 03 steps

How the forge works

PHASE_01 01

Go live with your stack

One command — forge cast — pipes your editor, your terminal, and your AI assistant session into a single broadcast. No overlays, no scene setup. Your messy real workspace is the show.

PHASE_02 02

Prompt in the open

Viewers see the prompt exactly as you wrote it, the model's raw output as it streams, and the diff as you accept, reject, or butcher it. The prompt log is indexed and searchable after the stream ends.

PHASE_03 03

Anyone forks the repo

Every stream is backed by a live repository. One click and a viewer forks the exact state of the code at any timestamp — including the prompt history that produced it. Remix culture, applied to software.
