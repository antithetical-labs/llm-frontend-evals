// 01 — how it works

Three steps. No magic.

01

Go live with your assistant

Start a stream from your editor. Your prompts, the model's output, and your diffs are captured as one timeline — no screen-sharing blur, no "trust me, it worked."

02

Viewers follow the reasoning

The audience sees the actual conversation driving the code. They can replay any moment, inspect any diff, and chat inline against a specific prompt or commit.

03

Fork the repo, keep the context

Every stream is backed by a real repository. Run forge fork @kira_builds/ratelimit and you get the code plus the full prompt history that produced it.
