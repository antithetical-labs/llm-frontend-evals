The Development Loop, Broadcasted.

We integrate directly with your environment to broadcast not just your screen, but the raw data of your session.

01.

Hook Up Your IDE

Connect your local editor via the Streamforge CLI. We securely sync your file tree, terminal output, and AI extension chat logs without exposing secrets.

02.

Broadcast The Flow

Go live. Your audience sees a structured, high-resolution view of your workspace. No more squinting at compressed 1080p screen shares to read code.

03.

Audience Forks Along

Every keystroke and AI generation is synced to our ephemeral git backend. Viewers can pull the exact repository state at any timestamp of the stream.

Prompt-to-Code Transparency

Watching senior developers work with LLMs is the fastest way to learn modern software engineering. We don't just show the final code; we expose the exact prompts, system context, and iterative refinements the streamer uses to coax the right output from the model.
