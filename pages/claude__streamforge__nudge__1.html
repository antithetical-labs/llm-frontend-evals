/ how it works

Four steps. No OBS scene collection required.

STEP 01

Install the CLI

sf go in your project root. It hooks your agent's stdin/stdout and your git working tree. That's the whole setup.

STEP 02

Work like normal

Prompts, tool calls, diffs and test output stream out as structured events — not a video of your screen. Secrets and .env are redacted by default.

STEP 03

Viewers scrub the timeline

Every turn is a timestamped node. Jump to "the moment it started working," read the exact prompt, diff any two points.

STEP 04

Anyone forks it

One click clones the repo at that node — plus the full conversation as context you can hand straight to your own assistant.

/ what makes it different

Built for people who read diffs for a living.

01

The prompt log is a first-class artifact

Video is a terrible format for code. Streamforge captures text as text: every prompt, every retry, every tool invocation, searchable and permalinkable. Quote turn 14 in your PR review.
