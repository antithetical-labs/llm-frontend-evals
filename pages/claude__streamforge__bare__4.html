How it works

Three moving parts. No setup ceremony.

Install the CLI, point it at a repo, and start talking to your assistant. Streamforge handles capture, sync, and playback.

01

Pipe your session

streamforge go wraps your editor and assistant, capturing prompts, completions, tool calls, and terminal output as a single timeline.

02

Viewers see all three lanes

Prompt lane, model output lane, and a live diff of the repo — scrubbable, searchable, and synced to the second.

03

Anyone forks and continues

One click clones the repo at that exact timestamp, prompt history included, so a fork starts with full context — not a blank buffer.

Built for people who ship

Not a webcam pointed at a monitor.

Streamforge treats the session as structured data, which is what makes everything below possible.
