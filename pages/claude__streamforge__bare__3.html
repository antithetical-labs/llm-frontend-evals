How it works

Three commands from terminal to audience.

Streamforge hooks into your existing setup. No screen recorder, no OBS scene graph, no separate repo.

Attach the agent

Run forge attach in your project. It taps your AI assistant's prompt/response channel and your git working tree — nothing else leaves your machine.

Go live

Viewers get three synced panes: your prompts, the model's streaming output, and a live diff view. Every commit is timestamped to the chat.

Anyone can fork

One click clones the repo at whatever commit is on screen, with the full prompt history included as a transcript file. Watchers leave with working code.

Built for people who ship

Not a webcam with syntax highlighting.

Streamforge treats the repository — not the video — as the primary artifact.

⑂

Forkable at any frame

Scrub the timeline, land on the moment the bug got fixed, and fork from exactly there. Prompts, diffs, and test output travel with the clone.

Secrets stay home

A local redaction pass strips .env values, tokens, and any path you glob-ignore before a single byte hits the wire. Panic key kills the feed in 200 ms.

forge.toml → redact = [".env*", "**/*.pem"]
