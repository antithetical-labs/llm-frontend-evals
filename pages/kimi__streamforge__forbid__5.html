How it works

From prompt to pull request, in public

Streamforge captures the whole loop — what the developer asks, what the model returns, and what actually ships.

1

Go live with your editor

One command connects your terminal and editor. Viewers see your prompts, the model's streaming output, and your diffs side by side.

2

Build with your AI pair

Work normally with Claude, GPT, or your local model. Every exchange is annotated on the timeline so viewers can jump to the moment a decision was made.

3

Viewers fork and follow

Any stream's repo can be forked with one click — including the full prompt history, so forks carry the reasoning, not just the code.
