how it works

Not a tutorial. Not a talk. A working session, live.

01 / broadcast

Go live from your editor

One CLI command or our OBS plugin. We capture your editor, terminal, and the assistant pane — prompts included, secrets automatically redacted.

→
02 / build

Build in public with your AI

Viewers see prompts, model responses, diffs, and test runs as separate, scrubbable tracks. They can rewind to the exact prompt that fixed the bug.

→
03 / fork

Viewers fork and continue

Every stream is backed by a real repo. Anyone watching can fork at any commit, replay the session offline, or open a PR against your live branch.

features

Built for developers who ship, not influencers who pose.

⟨/⟩

Prompt-track replay

Every stream records prompts, model outputs, and code diffs as separate timelines. Scrub to any moment and see exactly what was asked, what the model returned, and what you kept or threw away.

supports: Claude · GPT · Gemini · local models
⑂

One-click repo forking

The repo behind every stream is first-class. Fork it mid-broadcast, check out any historical commit from the session, or branch off the exact moment the streamer took a wrong turn — and show them why.

git-native · GitHub / GitLab / self-hosted
▣

Zero-leak streaming

Our local agent scans frames and terminal output before anything leaves your machine. API keys, .env files, and private hostnames are masked in real time — you approve the redaction rules once.

on-device redaction · SOC 2 in progress
