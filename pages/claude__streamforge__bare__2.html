How it works

Three moving parts. No production ceremony.

Point the CLI at a repo, wire up your assistant, and go. Streamforge handles capture, sync, and the fork button.

01

Attach the CLI

One command in your project root. Streamforge tails your assistant's session — prompts, tool calls, responses — and mirrors your working tree over an encrypted channel. Secrets and .env paths are excluded by default.

02

Broadcast the loop

Viewers get three synced panes: the prompt thread, the model's raw output, and a live diff of the code. Scrub back to any moment to see exactly which prompt produced which line.

03

Anyone forks it

A fork captures the repo at that exact frame plus the full prompt history as context. Clone it, hand it to your own assistant, take the build somewhere else.

Built for people who ship

Not a webcam pointed at a terminal.

Streamforge understands repos, prompts and diffs as first-class objects — so the stream is searchable, replayable, and executable.

⑂

Fork at any frame

Grab the tree as it existed at 01:42:07, with the reasoning that got it there. Forks arrive with a CONTEXT.md your assistant can pick up cold.

$ streamforge fork mira/auth-layer --at 01:42:07
