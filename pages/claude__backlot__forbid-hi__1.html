How it works

Three steps. Roughly one coffee.

You don't set up a chart of accounts. You forward an email and get on with the edit.

1

Forward the deal email

Send the brand's brief, PDF or DocuSign link to deals@backlot. Or drag it in. Or connect Gmail once and never think about it again.

Works with PDFs, Docs and screenshots.
2

We pull out the bits that matter

Fee, deliverables, deadlines, usage rights, exclusivity, payment terms — lifted out of the legalese and laid out on one card you can actually read.

You confirm. Takes about 20 seconds.
3

Backlot nags, not you

Deliverable dates hit your calendar. Invoices go out on the day they're allowed to. Late payers get politely chased without you writing the awkward email.

You just get a Monday summary.

The three things that quietly cost creators money.

01 · Contracts

Every brand deal, in plain English

Backlot reads the contract and tells you what you actually agreed to: how long they can run your face in a paid ad, which competitors you're locked out of, and what happens if the video slips a week.

  • Usage rights and exclusivity windows shown as dates, not clauses
  • Flags the stuff worth pushing back on before you sign
  • Signed copies stored per brand, searchable forever

02 · Invoices

Invoices that chase themselves

Raise an invoice from the deal you already logged — the numbers, PO reference and payment terms are filled in. Then Backlot follows up on day 7, 14 and 30 so you're not "just circling back" at midnight.

  • Reminders written to sound like a professional, not a beggar
  • See exactly who's late and by how many days
  • Multi-currency, VAT or no VAT, whichever you are
