How It Works

01

Connect your IDE

Install the Streamforge plugin for VS Code or JetBrains. It hooks into your AI extensions (Copilot, Cursor, Codeium) and captures your prompt telemetry automatically.

02

Stream your workflow

Go live. Streamforge renders a split-screen view for your audience natively on the web. No OBS required. Viewers see your editor state and your AI chats simultaneously.

03

The Audience Forks

Your workspace is synced to a temporary cloud environment. Viewers can click "Fork Current State" at any second of the broadcast to grab your exact code and prompt history.
