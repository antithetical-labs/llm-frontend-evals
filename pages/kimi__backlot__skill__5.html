How it works

Three steps, no spreadsheets.

Step 01

Connect your platforms

Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, and Patreon once. Backlot pulls in every payout automatically and keeps a running total of what you have actually earned.

Step 02

Drop in your deals

Forward a contract or add a brand deal in seconds. Backlot reads the deliverables, the fee, and the due dates, then puts them on your calendar.

Step 03

Get paid on time

Invoices go out when deliverables ship. If a brand goes quiet, Backlot nudges them for you, so you never have to write the awkward follow-up email.
