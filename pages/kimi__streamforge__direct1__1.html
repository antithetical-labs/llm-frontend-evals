Protocol 001 — How it works

Prompt. Stream. Fork.

Three steps between you and the most transparent way to learn — and teach — modern software development.

01

Go live with your stack

Connect your editor and your AI assistant. Your prompts, model outputs, and diffs broadcast as one unified stream — no extra tooling, just npx streamforge go.

02

Viewers watch the reasoning

Not just the code — the thinking. Every prompt you send and every response you accept or reject is visible, timestamped, and replayable.

03

Anyone forks the repo

One click clones the stream's repository at any point in the broadcast. Continue the build, remix the approach, or diff your version against the streamer's.
