How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

Set-up takes about the length of an export. After that, Backlot only speaks up when something needs you.

01

Drop the contract in

Forward the PDF or paste the email thread. Backlot pulls out the fee, the deliverables, the deadlines, the usage window and the exclusivity clause, and shows you what it found so you can correct it.

02

Connect where you get paid

Link YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitch and your bank. Platform payouts and brand transfers land on the same ledger, so "how much did I actually make in March" is one number, not an evening.

03

Work off the sheet

One screen tells you what ships this week, what's unpaid, and who needs a nudge. Invoices go out with two taps and chase themselves after that.

The work it takes off you

Built for one person doing all of it

No double-entry bookkeeping, no accounting vocabulary, no dashboard you have to learn. Production paperwork, for people who make videos.

Contracts, read for you

Every brand deal arrives as a different PDF with the important parts buried on page four. Backlot puts them on one line: what you owe, when, for how much, and what you promised not to do.

It flags the clauses that cost creators money — perpetual usage, category exclusivity, unpaid revisions, whitelisting rights — in words you already use.

Invoices that chase

Backlot writes the invoice from the deal you already logged, sends it to the right billing address, and then keeps asking. Polite reminder at day 7, firmer at 14, late-fee notice at 30 if your contract allows one.

You see one status per deal: sent, seen, overdue, paid. Nothing to reconcile by hand.

Payouts, per platform

AdSense pays monthly, the Creator Rewards Program pays on its own schedule, subs pay whenever they feel like it. Backlot tracks each stream separately and tells you what already cleared versus what's still projected.

Then it does the part nobody enjoys: a running estimate of what to hold back for tax, and a year-to-date summary your accountant will accept without a phone call.
