How it works

A standard video stream loses the context. Streamforge binds the video to the code state.

01

Connect your editor

Install the CLI plugin. We sync your active terminal, editor state, and AI copilot logs securely to your broadcast channel.

02

Broadcast the context

Viewers see a split screen: your webcam/voice, and a precise recreation of your IDE. They can read the code, not just watch pixels.

03

Viewers fork live

When a viewer spots an interesting pattern, they hit Fork. They get a clone of the repo exactly as it existed at that timestamp.

Engineered for code
