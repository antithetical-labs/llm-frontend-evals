How it works

From inbox chaos to one call sheet, in an afternoon.

No accounting degree required. No spreadsheet archaeology. Three steps and your channel has a back office.

Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Instagram, and the rest. Payouts sync automatically — no CSVs, no copy-paste, no “wait, did that ever hit my bank?”

Forward a contract or add a deal in seconds. Backlot pulls out the fee, the deliverables, and every sponsor deadline — and puts them on your calendar.

Invoices go out when deliverables ship, follow-ups send themselves, and every dollar lands on one dashboard. That’s a wrap on the busywork.
