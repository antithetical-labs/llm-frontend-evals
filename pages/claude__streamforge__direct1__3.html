01 / PROTOCOL

How it works

01

Plug in your editor

Install the Streamforge agent. It hooks your editor, terminal and AI assistant, then pipes prompts, completions and diffs to a live channel. One command, zero OBS scenes.

02

Broadcast the loop

Viewers get three synced panes: what you asked, what the model returned, and the code as it mutates. Chat can flag hallucinations before your tests do.

03

Fork any moment

Every stream is a git repo with a timeline. Scrub to minute 34, hit fork, and you get the working tree plus the exact prompt history that produced it.
