HOW THE FORGE WORKS

01

Go Live

Open your editor, pair it with your AI assistant, and hit broadcast. Your prompts, the model's output, and your keystrokes stream as one unified feed. Zero setup — run streamforge up in any repo.

02

Build In Public

Viewers watch the conversation between you and the model in real time — the dead ends, the refactors, the moment the tests go green. Chat reacts to diffs, not just webcams.

03

Get Forked

Every stream is backed by a live repository. Any viewer can fork the exact state of your code — mid-stream — and continue the build on their own branch of reality.
