/ How it works

The full build loop, broadcast from the source.

01 — CONNECT

Open your workspace.

Connect a repository and your AI coding assistant. Streamforge captures the build context without turning your setup into a studio.

$ streamforge live
02 — BUILD

Code like you normally do.

Your audience follows the prompts, model responses, file changes, tests, and commits as they happen—not in a cleaned-up recap.

03 — FORK

Turn viewers into builders.
