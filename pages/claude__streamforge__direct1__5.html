01 / how it works

Three steps to broadcast

Install the CLI, point it at your repo, and Streamforge captures your assistant session as a structured stream — not a screen recording.

01

Hook your session

npx streamforge live attaches to your editor and AI assistant. Prompts, completions, tool calls and file writes are captured as events — secrets are redacted before they leave your machine.

02

Viewers see everything

The player renders three synced panes: the prompt log, the streaming model output, and a live diff of the working tree. Scrub back to any moment and watch the reasoning that produced a line.

03

Anyone can fork

One click snapshots the repo at that exact commit — plus the full prompt history as context — into a fork under your account. Start where the stream is, not from a blank README.
