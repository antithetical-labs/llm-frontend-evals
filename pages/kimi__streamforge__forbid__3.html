How it works

From idea to forkable repo, in public.

No slides, no polished talks. Just real sessions — the messy, interesting middle of building software with an AI pair programmer.

01

Go live with your editor

Connect your editor and terminal in one click. Your prompts, the model's responses, and your diffs stream side by side — nothing staged.

02

Build with your AI assistant

Viewers watch you iterate: the prompt you write, the code the model proposes, and the edits you accept or reject. Chat can suggest prompts in real time.

03

Ship a forkable repo

Every stream is backed by a live repository. Anyone watching can fork the exact state of the code at any timestamp and continue from there.
