01How it worksThree moving parts. No editing, no post-production.
STEP 01

Someone starts a session

A developer opens Streamforge in their editor, points it at a repo, and goes live. Their assistant traffic is piped into the stream as structured events — not just a screen capture.

$ forge live --repo ./ledger-api ↳ session dt7k1 is live
STEP 02

You watch three panes at once

Left: every prompt, correction and swear word. Middle: the model's response as it streams. Right: the working tree, diffed live. Scrub back to any moment you missed.

[prompt] → [output] → [diff] ⏮ scrubbable · 30d retention
STEP 03

You fork and take over

Hit fork at any commit and you get the repo at that exact state, plus the full prompt transcript that produced it. Your fork, your assistant, your direction.

$ forge fork dt7k1@e4f19ac ↳ cloned + transcript.md
02What you actually getBuilt for people who ship, not people who collect bookmarks.
APrompt transparency

The prompt log is a first-class artifact.

Every stream produces a searchable transcript: which model, which context files, how many retries, what the human said when it went sideways. Search across all streams for the phrasing that actually worked.

Filter by model, language, framework, or "sessions where tests passed on the first run" (there are fewer than you'd hope).

search: "fix flaky playwright" ───────────────────────────── ▸ @dmitri claude · 4 retries · passed ▸ @nvo gpt · 1 retry · passed ▸ @sunhwa local 8b · 11 retries · gave up ↳ 3,102 transcripts matched
BForkable repos

Fork the stream, not a zip file.

Streams are backed by real git. Each fork carries commit history, the assistant's context window at that point, and the environment manifest — so it builds on your machine the same way it built on theirs.

Forks are attributed upstream. If your fork solves the thing the streamer was stuck on, they'll see it in their session panel while still live.

forge fork dt7k1@e4f19ac ───────────────────────────── ✓ 214 commits ✓ transcript.md (1.1 MB) ✓ .forge/context.lock ✓ devcontainer + env manifest ready in 6.2s → cd ledger-api
CStreamer controls

Go live without leaking your secrets.

A redaction layer sits between your assistant and the broadcast: env vars, tokens, customer data and any path you glob-exclude never leave your machine. Private repos can stream diffs-only.

Pause the feed with one keystroke, drop a 90-second delay, or run invite-only sessions for your team's pairing hours.

.forge/redact ───────────────────────────── ✗ .env* hidden ✗ **/fixtures/pii hidden ✗ Bearer sk-… masked ✓ src/** streaming delay 90s · mode: invite-only
