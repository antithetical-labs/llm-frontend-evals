How it works

From idea to forkable repo, in public.

Streamforge captures your entire AI pair-programming loop and publishes it as a living, forkable artifact.

01

Go live with one command

Run forge cast in any project. We stream your editor, your terminal, and your assistant conversation — synced to a real git repo.

02

Build with your AI, visibly

Viewers see the exact prompts you send, the raw model output, and the diff as you accept, reject, or rewrite. No polished demos — real work.

03

Anyone forks and continues

Every stream is backed by a live repository. Viewers fork at any commit, replay the prompt history, and keep building where you left off.

Features

Built for working developers.

Not a tutorial platform. A transparent record of how modern software actually gets made.

>_

Prompt-level replay

Scrub a timeline of every prompt and response. Pin the moment a tricky bug got solved, copy the exact prompt, and see why the model's first answer was rejected.

⑂

One-click repo forking

Each stream ships a real repository with full history. Fork from any commit mid-stream, open it in your editor, and the prompt log travels with it as STREAMLOG.md.

◉

Multi-model transparency

Tag which model produced which code. Compare how different assistants approach the same problem across streams, with benchmarks on accepted vs. rewritten output.
