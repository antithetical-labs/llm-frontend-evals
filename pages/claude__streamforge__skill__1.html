How a session runs

Four stages, in order. You install one thing and the rest is the work you were going to do anyway.

01

Attach the recorder

A small process sits between your editor, your assistant, and your repo. It reads the prompt log and the working tree. It does not read your shell history or your environment file.

02

Go live

Three panes go out together: what you typed, what the model returned, and the diff as it applies. Viewers scrub back through any of them without losing the live edge.

03

Field the room

Chat messages can carry a file path and line number. Click one and the panel jumps there. Good suggestions get promoted into your prompt queue with one keystroke.

04

Leave something behind

When you stop, the session freezes into a repository at the exact commit you ended on, with the prompt transcript stored alongside it. Anyone can fork both.

Boundaries

Redaction you can trust

Path and pattern rules are enforced in the recorder before anything leaves your machine, and the stream shows a marker where content was withheld.

redact:
  - "**/.env*"
  - "**/secrets/**"
  - /sk-[A-Za-z0-9]{20,}/
