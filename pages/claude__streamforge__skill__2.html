how it works

Three lanes, one clock. Everything stays in sync.

  1. 01

    Attach the agent

    Point the CLI at your editor session or coding agent. Streamforge taps the prompt log and the working tree — no screen capture, no reading 9pt text through a video codec.

  2. 02

    Go live

    Prompts, model responses, tool calls, and diffs stream as text on a shared timeline. Viewers scrub backwards without losing the thread.

  3. 03

    Viewers fork the timestamp

    Any second of the stream is a real commit. One click clones the tree exactly as it stood, prompt history included, and the fork stays linked to its origin.

privacy

Redaction that holds

Env files, tokens, and paths you name are stripped before the frame leaves your machine, and the same rules apply to replays and forks. Nothing to scrub after the fact.
