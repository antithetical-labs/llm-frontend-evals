How it works

Three steps, then it runs quietly in the background.

You already do the hard part — making the videos. Backlot handles the paperwork trail that comes after the upload.

1

Drop in the deal

Forward the email or drag the contract PDF in. Backlot pulls out the fee, deliverables, deadlines, usage window and exclusivity — and shows them as plain sentences you can actually read.

Takes ~30 seconds per deal
2

Connect your payouts

Link YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Patreon and your bank. Every incoming payment lands on one timeline, matched to the deal or platform it came from.

Read-only access. Nothing can be moved.
3

Get told what matters

One nudge a week: what's due, what's late, what to invoice. Invoices send themselves and follow up at 7, 14 and 30 days so you never have to write "just circling back".

Sunday recap, Monday to-do list
