How it works

Three steps. About twenty minutes. Then it runs itself.

You don't need to learn bookkeeping. You just need to know what's owed, what's late, and what you owe a sponsor by Friday.

1

Drop in the deal

Forward the sponsor email or upload the PDF. Backlot pulls out the fee, the deliverables, the usage window and the payment terms, and shows you what it found so you can fix anything odd.

2

Connect your platforms

Link the accounts that pay you. Every platform payout is logged automatically next to your brand-deal income, so your real monthly number stops being a guess.

3

Work the one list

Each morning you get a single list: deliverables due, invoices to send, payments gone quiet. Tap once to send a chase email in plain, polite English.
