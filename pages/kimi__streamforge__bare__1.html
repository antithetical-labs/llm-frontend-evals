// how it works

From blank file to deployed feature, in the open.

No polished tutorials. No jump cuts. Just the real loop of prompting, evaluating, and editing — as it happens.

01

Go live with your assistant

Connect your editor and your AI tool of choice. Your prompts, the model's output, and your diff stream side by side in one view.

02

Viewers follow the reasoning

The audience sees not just what the model suggested, but what you accepted, rejected, and rewrote — the judgment that actually ships software.

03

Anyone can fork the repo

Every stream is backed by a live repository. One click forks the exact state on screen, complete with the full prompt history as commit context.
