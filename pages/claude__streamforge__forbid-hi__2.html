How it works

Three panes, one session, a repo you can take with you.

Streamforge hooks into your editor and your assistant, then publishes the parts other developers actually want to see — not just your screen.

01

Connect your session

Install the CLI and run forge live. It attaches to your assistant's transcript and your git worktree — Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, Aider or a plain API loop.

02

Broadcast the loop

Viewers get the prompt, the streamed response, and the diff side by side, plus your terminal and test output. Secrets and .env files are redacted before the frame leaves your machine.

03

Anyone forks the state

Every commit is mirrored to a public repo pinned to the stream timeline. Hit F at minute 26 and you get the tree exactly as it stood at minute 26.
